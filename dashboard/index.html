How the value is worked out

Implied value = TVL × 0.30, times a growth multiplier (seven-day change clamped to −50%…+100%, weight 0.6), times a maturity multiplier (0.75–1.00 over four years tracked with no token; 180 days assumed when the listing date is unknown), times a breadth multiplier (1.00–1.30 for one to seven chains). The same function scores every row — no protocol is ever special-cased. Protocols whose token already trades are shown apart, at their market capitalisation, never at a modelled value.

Data: a frozen DefiLlama snapshot. “Records no token” means the snapshot carries no token for the protocol — it is not proof that none exists. Farm steps are a starting point, not financial advice; never pay to claim an airdrop, and check every link on the protocol's own page.
